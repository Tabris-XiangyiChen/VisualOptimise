A semi-realistic fantasy roguelike ruin used for testing UE Map Builder V2 mesh placement.
The map contains connected stone wall segments, full wall blocks, half walls, fixed doorway structures, KayKit floor meshes, dirt ground, grass, wooden bridge, water, doors, stairs, and a slope.
The layout is a controlled modular test scene with straight walls, corners, T junctions, a cross junction, door alignment, a half-wall underlay test, full tile stairs, and a single slope.
Use muted grey stone, dark dirt, mossy green grass, aged wood, and blue-green water. Keep the material surfaces readable and suitable for World Aligned projection in UE5.
